Speak to Type

An Android app for voice notes. You talk and it writes down what you say. The free version has one small banner ad. There is no account and no subscription.

Get it on Google Play

Why I made it

I used a dictation notes app on my old phone. It capped me at 10 notes and the ads got in the way. I wanted something I could actually use, so I built it. Speak to Type has no note limit, shows one small banner ad, and doesn't ask you to sign up for anything.

I'm one person. I built this and run it under the name Clear Elm. You can email me at clearelm.app@gmail.com.

How it works

Tap the mic and talk. Your words appear as a note. It keeps listening while you pause, so you don't restart it after every sentence. By default it stops after 45 seconds of silence. You can turn that off for free. You can edit a note after you dictate it, and copy the text out.

Common questions

Is it really free?

Yes. Unlimited notes, no payment needed. The free version shows one small banner ad, and Premium takes it away.

Is there a subscription?

No. Premium is a one-time purchase.

Is it ad-free?

Not the free version. It has one small banner. There are no fullscreen ads and nothing shows up while you dictate. Premium removes the banner.

Does it cut off when I pause?

No. It keeps listening through pauses and stops after 45 seconds of silence by default. You can turn the auto-stop off for free, or set your own timeout with Premium.

Do I need an account?

No. Your notes are saved on your phone.

What happens to my audio?

I don't run a server and I never receive your notes or your voice. The app uses Android's speech recognition, and on some phones that sends audio to Google. The free version's ad comes from Google AdMob. The privacy policy has the details.
